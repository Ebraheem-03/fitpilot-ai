# LLM plan-generation spike, 2026-10-28 (M3 S0)

Evidence for [ADR-0006](../decisions/0006-zero-cost-hosted-llm-provider.md). Throwaway script: `services/api/scripts/spikes/llm_plan_spike.py` (prompt `plan-spike-v1`). Aggregates only. The raw per-call JSONL stayed local because it holds generated plans and nothing else of interest. **Synthetic profiles only, no PII, no keys.**

## Setup

- **Provider:** Groq free tier, OpenAI-compatible `chat/completions`, `response_format: json_schema` with `strict: true`, `include_reasoning: false`, `max_completion_tokens: 6000`, default temperature. No tools (Groq can't combine them with structured output).
- **Catalogue:** the hypothetical S1 catalogue, 7 exercises. `bodyweight_squat`, `push_up`, `glute_bridge`, `reverse_lunge` (reps) and `plank` (duration) need no equipment; `dumbbell_biceps_curl` and `dumbbell_row` need dumbbells. `exercise_id` is a JSON-schema `enum` of those ids.
- **Profiles:** 12 synthetic profiles covering every goal and experience level, 1–6 days/week, 10–180 min sessions, and equipment sets none / dumbbells / dumbbells+bench+mat / kettlebell+band (equipment the catalogue doesn't use) / mat.
  - Edge cases: 1 day × 90 min; 6 days × 10 min (the minimum); 180 min (the maximum); muscle gain with no equipment; days and minutes unset (backend defaults 3 days × 45 min); 6 days × 15 min.
- **Validation (in order):**
  1. Pydantic `TrainingPlan` (types, ranges: sets 1–6, reps 1–30, duration_s 10–180, rest_s 15–240, 1–8 exercises a day, string lengths).
  2. `exercise_id` is in the catalogue.
  3. Equipment is a subset of the member's equipment.
  4. Number of days equals `days_per_week`, and the days are distinct.
  5. Reps vs duration matches the catalogue prescription.
  6. No duplicate exercise within a day.
  7. Estimated session length ≤ `session_minutes`, with `estimate = 5 + Σ sets × (work_s + rest_s) / 60` and `work_s = 3 × reps` or `duration_s`.
- **Repair:** one attempt. The repair call sends the original messages, the rejected output (if any) and the error list. An HTTP 400 from Groq counts as a failed first try, and the repair call gets the API error text.
- **Pacing:** one thread per model, because limits are per model. The pacer sleeps until `x-ratelimit-reset-tokens` when fewer than 4,000 TPM remain. On a 429 it honours `retry-after`, and gives up when `retry-after` exceeds 120 s (the daily cap).

Shared observations:

- **Every failure was a Groq HTTP 400 `json_validate_failed`; no failure was a domain violation.** 120b had 2 failed calls, 20b had 11.
- An independent re-check of every accepted plan found 0 violations of days/week, equipment or session length. Every profile with dumbbells got at least one dumbbell exercise (120b 15/15, 20b 13/13). The kettlebell/band profile never got one.

- **120b, low effort:** no failures. The maximum completion was 2,467 tokens, so truncation is no longer a risk. The median reasoning was 381 tokens. One plan had a day under 50% fill.
- **20b, low effort:** all 4 first-try failures were **daily-cap 429s** (`retry-after` 274–1,715 s) after about 165k tokens that day. None was a quality failure, and every 20b plan that got a response was valid. This is a capacity result, not a quality one.

## Representative failure cases (main run)

- **Truncation.** `120b` p04 (6 days): `json_validate_failed: max completion tokens reached before generating a valid document` (reasoning plus 6 days of JSON exceeded 6,000 tokens). The repair, a fresh generation with the error fed back, was valid.
- **Wrong top-level shape.** `120b` p04: `jsonschema: '' does not validate with /type: expected object, but got array` (the model emitted the days array as the root). Repaired.
- **Misplaced field.** `20b` p04: `/days/5 … additionalProperties 'rationale' not allowed` (rationale put inside the last day). The repair was then truncated, so the plan was escalated.
- **Generic failure.** `20b` p01/p03/p10: `json_validate_failed: Failed to generate JSON` (no detail). Repaired.

## Limits observed

- **Headers on the free organisation:** `x-ratelimit-limit-requests: 1000` (per day) and `x-ratelimit-limit-tokens: 8000` (per minute), the same for both models. These match the Developer-plan base table in Groq's rate-limit docs (30 RPM, 1K RPD, 8K TPM, 200K TPD).
- **TPM:** at about 3.8k tokens per plan, 8K TPM allows **about 2 plans a minute per model**. The pacer kept 429s to 5 in 72 plans, and all of them were recovered.
- **TPD:** this is the binding limit. 20b hit the daily cap (`retry-after` up to 29 min) after about 165k tokens. At 200K TPD, 120b supports about **50 plans a day at medium effort, or about 80 at low**. An S5 eval pass over 20 profiles uses roughly 50–75k tokens, which is 25–40% of a day.
- **Paid-equivalent cost** at Groq list prices ($0.15 in / $0.60 out per 1M tokens for 120b): about $0.0018 per plan at medium and $0.0010 at low. The free tier bills $0.
